Custom website built for your business. Mobile-first, fast-loading,

Includes: Home page, services page, contact/lead form, Google
Analytics, basic SEO setup.

The full infrastructure retainer. Website + GBP optimization + lead
capture + monthly updates and reporting.

Includes: Everything in Launch, Google Business Profile setup and
optimization, monthly site edits, lead tracking, monthly performance
snapshot.

Best for: Service businesses ready to be found locally and capture

## Tier 3 — Growth | $797/month

Foundation plus AI-powered automation layer.

Includes: Everything in Foundation, automated lead follow-up sequences,
missed call text-back, review request automation, AI chatbot or
booking integration.

Best for: Businesses with consistent lead flow who are losing jobs
because they can't follow up fast enough.

## Tier 4 — Systems | Custom quote

Full business infrastructure buildout. Dashboards, AI agents, custom
tools, operations automation.

Includes: Scoped per client. Setup fee $2,500–$5,000+. Monthly
management negotiated separately.

Best for: Established businesses that want to run on technology, not
on the owner's memory.

website that doesn't do anything. RAM builds the infrastructure that
