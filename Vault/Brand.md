# RAM Strategic Systems — Visual Brand

Confirmed from the real site (ram-strategicsystems.com) via screenshot,
2026-08-18. See also `RAM-Voice.md` for tone/copy voice.

**Important correction:** the "Design System (RAM projects)" section in
the old Claw-generated context brief was wrong for this purpose — those
details (navy/gold/parchment, Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display) are
Ovis Canem's KJV-study aesthetic, not RAM Strategic Systems' own brand.
Don't reuse that section for anything RAM-facing.

## Colors

- Background: near-black, `#0a0a0f`
- Card/elevated surface: `#16161d`, subtle border `#26262f`
- Accent: indigo/blue-violet, approx `#4f46e5`
- Headline text: white
- Body/muted text: gray, approx `#9ca3af`
- Subtle dot-grid texture on the dark background

## Type

- Headlines: bold, rounded-geometric sans-serif (site uses something in
  the Sora/Space Grotesk family)
- Body/UI: clean sans-serif (Inter or similar)

## Layout language

- Stat cards: icon top-left in accent color, big bold number, small gray
  description below, rounded corners (~12-16px), bordered
- Buttons: solid indigo fill, white text, moderate rounding (not full pill)
- Overall feel: modern SaaS/startup energy — confident, dark-mode,
  minimal, not corporate or agency-like. Matches the voice doc's "never
  sound like an agency."

## Used for

The RAM-OS dashboard (`site/build.js`) was rebuilt to match this, 2026-08-18.
